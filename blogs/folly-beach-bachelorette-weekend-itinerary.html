A 3-Day Folly Beach Bachelorette Weekend Itinerary

Home › Blog › 3-Day Weekend Itinerary

November 16, 2026

Three days is the sweet spot for Folly. It's enough time for a beach day, a boat day, and two big nights without anyone needing a vacation from the vacation. This plan keeps rides short and leaves room for naps. For every restaurant, charter, and bar in one place, see our Folly Beach bachelorette party guide.

Friday: Arrive, Settle In, Marsh Sunset

Check into the rental, hand out the matching tees, and head off-island for a relaxed first dinner. Bowens Island Restaurant is a no-frills seafood spot on its own island off Folly Road and the area's go-to for sunset over the marsh. Lolo's, a small house on the Folly River about a mile before the bridge, has water views from every table. Back on the island, finish with ice cream at Dolce Banana Café and an early night.

Saturday: Water in the Morning, Boat in the Afternoon

Start with coffee at Roasted inside the Tides hotel, then split the group. Surfers can book a lesson with Shaka Surf School or Isla Surf School, and everyone else can paddle the Folly River with Charleston SUP Safaris. Refuel at Taco Boy.

Late afternoon is boat time. A three-hour private pontoon with Flipper Finders holds up to 18 and includes a cooler and ice. Shower, then dinner at Pinky's on the Beach for the pink-booth photos. Close the night with the Center Street bar crawl, or keep it at the house with a private bachelorette show.

Sunday: Pier Brunch and a Lighthouse Goodbye

Brunch at Pier 101, right on the Folly Beach Pier, or Lost Dog Café if you get there early. Walk out from the east end to see the Morris Island Lighthouse for the last group photo, then pack up.
