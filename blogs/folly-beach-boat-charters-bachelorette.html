Private Boat Charters for a Folly Beach Bachelorette

Home › Blog › Private Boat Charters

November 12, 2026

If the group only books one activity, make it a boat. It's the one time all weekend everyone is in the same place, the drinks are cold, and the dolphins show up on cue. Here's how the Folly options compare. For everything else on the island, start with our Folly Beach bachelorette party guide.

Party Pontoons for Big Groups

Flipper Finders, based on Center Street, runs private three-hour party pontoon charters for up to 18 people with a cooler and ice included. Their Dolphin Queen holds up to 20 and stops at a nearby beach so you can swim and float. Some boats have no bathroom, so use the office before you board.

Tiki Boats and Sunset Sails for Smaller Crews

A private tiki boat fits up to 12 and turns the river into a floating bar. For something calmer, book a sunset sail on a catamaran from the Folly River landing. It's the prettiest hour of the day and the easiest group photo of the weekend.

Morris Island Charters for BYOB Dolphin Cruises

Morris Island Charters runs private sunset and dolphin cruises from 97 Center Street, and you can bring your own drinks. Many trips stop at Morris Island to look for shells and shark teeth near the lighthouse.

Booking Tips

Book the boat right after the house, since spring through fall weekends fill fast. Many charters only give a full refund if you cancel three weeks out. Schedule it for late afternoon, after the morning paddle or surf lesson, and pack reef-safe sunscreen and hats, since there's little shade on the water.
