The Guide

Where to Eat & What to Do for a Bachelorette in Folly Beach

Chill by day, celebrate by night. Here are the picks worth your group's time.

Best Places to Eat

Brunch

  • Lost Dog Café: Pancakes, strong coffee, and mimosas. Go early on weekends.
  • Rita's Seaside Grille: Coastal plates and live music, steps from the sand.

Dinner

  • BLU Beach Bar & Grill: Oceanfront seafood and cocktails. Book near sunset.
  • Jack of Cups Saloon: A quirky Center Street spot with bold dishes and craft drinks.
  • The Crab Shack: Laid-back seafood buckets with no dress code.

Things to Do

Daytime

  • Beach day by the pier: Close to food, bathrooms, and parking.
  • Paddleboard or kayak: Marsh creeks, calm water, and dolphin sightings.
  • Group spa day: Massages and facials to recharge before the night.
  • Morris Island Lighthouse: Walk out from the east end for the group photo.

Nighttime

  • Private show at your rental: The night's kickoff, all about the bride.
  • Sunset cruise: A private boat, champagne, and a pink sky.
  • Sunset beach picnic: Snacks and music by the ocean. Fires aren't allowed on the beach.

Center Street

Top Bars for a Bachelorette Party on Folly Beach

Folly's nightlife is lively but low-key, and it's all within walking distance. Start with the private show at your place, then head out while the energy's high.

  • Snapper Jacks: A rooftop with ocean views and DJs. It's the place to start the night.
  • Loggerhead's Beach Grill: Tropical drinks and easygoing vibes.
  • The Washout: Loud music and big energy, ready to party.
  • The Bounty Bar: A beachy spot for dancing and a nightcap.

Stress-Free Planning

Folly Beach Bachelorette Tips

A little planning goes a long way. Here's what every maid of honor should know.

Book earlyLock in the house first, then the private show and dinner reservations.
No alcohol or glass on the beachSave toasts for the rental, the boat, or the bar. Bring reusable cups.
Getting aroundUber from downtown to Center Street. Bikes or golf carts work on the island, and paid lots are near the pier.
Where to stayA house or condo walkable to Center Street keeps rides cheap and doubles as your show venue.
What to packSwimsuits, reef-safe sunscreen, towels, walkable sandals, and matching tees or sashes.
Budget as a groupSet a per-person number early and split the house and show evenly.
