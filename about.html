Folly Beach, SC · About

About Us

A simple, honest guide for groups planning a bachelorette party on Folly Beach, South Carolina.

Who We Are

We're an independent planning resource for brides, maids of honor, and friend groups heading to Folly Beach for a celebration weekend. We put the essentials in one place: where to eat, how to get on the water, where to go out, and the practical details that keep the weekend running smoothly.

What We Do

We keep the guide short and useful. Instead of endless lists, we highlight spots and experiences that work well for groups, plus tips on getting around, following local beach rules, and booking early enough to get what you want.

Our Approach

  • We focus on what matters for groups: walkability, atmosphere, and ease.
  • We keep information simple and as current as we can.
  • We're independent and not affiliated with the restaurants, bars, or outfitters we mention.

A Quick Note

Hours, menus, prices, and local rules change. Always confirm details directly with a business before you go. If something is out of date, let us know and we'll take a look.
