About Us

About Us

A simple, honest guide for groups planning a bachelorette party on Folly Beach, South Carolina.

Who We Are

We are an independent planning resource for brides, maids of honor, and friend groups heading to Folly Beach for a celebration weekend. We know how much goes into planning a group trip, so we put the essentials in one place: where to eat, what to do, where to go out, and the practical details that keep the weekend running smoothly.

What We Do

We keep our guide short and useful. Instead of endless lists, we highlight a handful of spots and experiences that work well for groups, plus tips on getting around, staying on the right side of local beach rules, and booking early enough to get what you want.

Our Approach

  • We focus on what matters for groups: walkability, atmosphere, and ease.
  • We keep information simple and up to date as best we can.
  • We feature only a few experiences we think are worth your time.
  • We're independent and not affiliated with the restaurants or bars we mention.

A Quick Note

Hours, menus, prices, and local rules can change. Always confirm details directly with a business before you go. If you notice something that's out of date, let us know and we'll take a look.
